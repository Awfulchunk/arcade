HOW TO PLAY
  1. BUILD ANYWHERE: hit the 🔨 hammer (or tap empty grass in your base), pick a building, drag the ghost where you want it. Tap ✓ PLACE. A little worker walks out and builds it.
  2. BUILDINGS = RESOURCES: Barracks makes Manpower, Forge Steel, Workshop Parts, Armory Ammo, Field Hospital heals your stuff. Your HQ drips a little of everything.
  3. TAP A BUILDING to upgrade it, move it, or sell it. Forge, Armory and Barracks also sell troop armor, damage and speed upgrades.
  4. DEPLOY: tap a card, then tap the green zone behind your front wall. Some cards are a whole squad.
  5. ORDERS: 🚩 RALLY then tap the map: troops gather there. ⚔ CHARGE sends everyone. ↩ RETREAT pulls everyone back home.
  6. PICK A GROUP: tap a troop to pick it, double-tap to pick every nearby troop of that type, or hit ⬚ SELECT (or hold empty ground) and drag a box. Then tap the map: only that group goes there, around walls. 1 2 3 squad buttons save a group: tap to pick it again, hold to overwrite.
  7. HOLD a troop or building for the action ring: upgrade, rally, sell, and stances ⚔ Aggressive · ✋ Hold Ground · 🛡 Defensive · ↩ Retreat.
  8. OUTPOSTS: their HQ has a 🛡 shield until all of their forward outposts fall. Each outpost has a gun, a wall ring and calls out defenders when hit. Yours protect you the same way.
  9. CREATURES: wolves, bandits and a huge beast in the middle hide in the fog and attack anyone. Kill them for loot. 🔭 Scouts are cheap and quick, creatures mostly ignore them, and their 🎆 flare lights a big area for 10s. A 🗼 Watchtower reveals its area forever.
  10. FOG OF WAR: you only see near your troops and buildings. Scout before you charge.
  11. THE WALL: their wall blocks everything. Smash a hole (Hank, Ken, Mortars), take the outposts, then pour through to their HQ. Kill the HQ = win.
  12. SUPPORT GUYS DON'T FIGHT: Sandbag Sam drops cover, Trap Daddy plants mines and spike pits, Duct Tape Dave builds a turret that overheats, Brick Shithouse builds a wall that pushes your territory forward.
  13. OVERTIME: at 20:00 it's sudden death (walls rot, cash x1.8). At 25:00 most HQ health wins.
  14. DRAG to scroll, PINCH to zoom. Tap the strip on the right to jump.
